Petra Wadi Rum

From Petra

✕No public transport ~1 h 45 min drive

Wadi Rum is not bookable in JETT's booking system (checked 24 Sep 2026) although the JETT homepage lists it.

Sunset jeep tours need arrival before ~16:00.

  • Pre-arranged transfer Recommended

    ~1 h 45 min

    Book through your camp or a licensed driver; fixed price agreed in advance. Camps quote about 40 JOD per car (sedan, up to 4 people).

    est. 35–45 JOD per car

  • Rum Bus shared shuttle (Wadi Musa 06:00–06:30)

    ~2 h 30 min

    One operator's own shuttle, leaves early, you lose the Petra morning; arrives Wadi Rum ~09:00.

    est. 10 JOD per person

  • Taxi from Wadi Musa

    ~1 h 45 min

    Agree the fare before leaving; availability varies in the afternoon.

    Negotiated on the day

  • Bus via Aqaba + taxi

    Much longer

    Adds a change in Aqaba; not practical for this day.

    Lower fare, extra taxi

  • Rental car

    ~1 h 30 min

    Drivers must be 25+.

    est. 25–30 JOD per day + fuel

Wadi Rum Aqaba

To Aqaba

!Limited public transport ~1 h drive

  • Taxi / driver Recommended

    ~1 h

    About 25 JOD per car to Aqaba city; 35 JOD to Aqaba airport (Wadi Rum Nomads). Public midibus Aqaba–Wadi Rum exists (LTRC tariff 5.10 JOD) but no timetable.

    est. 25–30 JOD
