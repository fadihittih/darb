Amman عمّان

Plan 3 h+

Amman Citadel: 3 JOD · Jordan Pass ✓ · verified 30 Sep 2026.

Autumn: 26°C day, 14°C night. Sun hat and light layers; comfortable shoes for the hills.

How do I get from Queen Alia Airport (AMM) to Amman without a car?

Airport taxi: ~45 min, est. 20–25 JOD (estimate, not verified). Fixed-fare desk inside arrivals.

How do I get from Amman to Petra without a car?

JETT bus Abdali → Wadi Musa: departs 06:30, 4 h, 10 JOD ✓ verified 24 Sep 2026 (source: jett.com.jo booking system). One daily departure — miss it and the day is gone.

How do I get from Amman to Jerash without a car?

Taxi / driver: ~50 min, est. 20–30 JOD (estimate, not verified).

How do I get from Amman to Dead Sea without a car?

There is no scheduled public transport on this route. Best option: Taxi / driver: ~1 h, est. 25–35 JOD (estimate, not verified).

How do I get from Amman to Madaba & Mount Nebo without a car?

Taxi / driver: ~45 min, est. 15–20 JOD (estimate, not verified).

How do I get from Amman to Umm Qais without a car?

Private driver: ~2 h, est. 45–60 JOD (estimate, not verified).

How do I get from Amman to Dana Biosphere Reserve without a car?

There is no scheduled public transport on this route. Best option: Private driver: ~3 h, est. 70–90 JOD (estimate, not verified).

How do I get from Amman to Aqaba without a car?

JETT bus Amman → Aqaba: ~4 h 30 min, est. 8–12 JOD (estimate, not verified). Fare and times to be confirmed in JETT's booking system.

How do I get from Amman to Kerak Castle without a car?

Private driver: ~2 h, est. 40–55 JOD (estimate, not verified).

Petra البترا

Plan 6 h+

Petra (1 day): 50 JOD · Jordan Pass ✓ · verified 30 Sep 2026. 50/55/60 JOD for 1/2/3 days; 90 JOD for same-day visitors with no overnight stay

Autumn: 26°C day, 13°C night. Walking shoes — 8+ km on foot, 2 L water.

How do I get from Amman to Petra without a car?

JETT bus Abdali → Wadi Musa: departs 06:30, 4 h, 10 JOD ✓ verified 24 Sep 2026 (source: jett.com.jo booking system). One daily departure — miss it and the day is gone.

How do I get from Petra to Wadi Rum without a car?

Wadi Rum is not bookable in JETT's booking system (checked 24 Sep 2026) although the JETT homepage lists it. Best option: Pre-arranged transfer: ~1 h 45 min, est. 35–45 JOD per car (estimate, not verified). Book through your camp or a licensed driver; fixed price agreed in advance. Sunset jeep tours need arrival before ~16:00.

How do I get from Petra to Aqaba without a car?

Private driver: ~2 h, est. 45–60 JOD (estimate, not verified).

How do I get from Dana Biosphere Reserve to Petra without a car?

There is no scheduled public transport on this route. Best option: Private driver: ~1 h 30 min, est. 40–55 JOD (estimate, not verified). Mountain road via Shobak — slower than the distance suggests.
