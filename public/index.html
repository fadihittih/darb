Why plans break in Jordan

Looks great on paper. Falls apart on the road.

No bus where you need it

JETT’s booking system lists one daily bus to Petra (06:30, 10 JOD) and no Wadi Rum route at all. Without a car, some legs need a pre-arranged transfer — and a budget for it.

Days that don’t fit

Jerash and the Dead Sea in one afternoon looks fine in a list. On real roads it’s hours of driving with no direct bus.

Money left on the table

The Jordan Pass can save around 41 JOD versus buying the visa and tickets separately — if you stay 2+ nights.

Jordan Pass

Is the Jordan Pass worth it for your trip?

It bundles your tourist visa with entry to Petra, Jerash and other major sites — but the visa is only waived if you stay at least 2 nights.

Darb prices it against your actual days, counting only verified ticket prices.
