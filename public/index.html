The rules

  • Stay at least 2 nights (3 days)Only then is the visa fee waived, shorter stays pay the visa on top.
  • Buy it online before you landA Pass bought after arrival does not waive the visa.
  • Petra days set the tierCount the days you spend inside Petra and pick the tier that matches.
